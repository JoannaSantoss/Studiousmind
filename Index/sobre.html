About StudiousMind

A free place to build knowledge and manage your study time.

What is StudiousMind?

StudiousMind is a study platform focused on two things: learning new skills and using your time well. You choose a course, follow it at your own pace and keep track of your progress.

Why is it open source?

The code is public on GitHub, so anyone can read it, suggest improvements or use it to learn how a website is built.

How can I support the project?

  1. Open the repository on GitHub.
  2. Click the star button to mark it as a favorite.
  3. Share it with someone who is learning to code.
